Our History

Sadat Academy for Management Sciences (SAMS) is a private educational institution in Egypt. It was established in 1981 by the late President Mohamed Anwar El-Sadat. The academy offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in various fields of study, including computer science, management sciences, and languages and translation. SAMS is known for its high-quality education and research facilities, as well as its commitment to developing future leaders with knowledge and innovation.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide high-quality education and research opportunities that empower students to become future leaders in their fields. We are committed to fostering a culture of innovation, creativity, and critical thinking that prepares students to meet the challenges of the 21st century. Through our diverse programs and dedicated faculty, we aim to inspire students to pursue excellence and make a positive impact on society.
